The web, adapted to you.

Cancelling a service, requesting a certificate, finding a branch's opening hours. Simple tasks that turn into a maze on many sites. The site owner rarely fixes it, and the people who suffer it can't do anything about it.

How it works

Before touching a site, four agents study it

Every change comes from watching someone get lost, not from an opinion about how the page should look.

  1. 01 Cataloger

    Finds out what people come for

    Reads the site and catalogs what people come to do on it: the procedures, the questions, what they look for.

  2. 02 Guide

    Finds the shortest path

    For each goal it searches for and verifies the reference route: how to get there, page by page.

  3. 03 Visitors

    They try it like people

    Synthetic users attempt the task seeing only screenshots, on desktop and on a phone: some go straight, others start from the menu, the search box or the footer.

  4. 04 Designer

    Generates the theme

    With what the attempts show, it writes a theme: more contrast, the task in plain sight, shortcuts where people got lost.

Path map

See where people get lost

Every visitor's attempt is drawn as it happens: the recommended route as the axis, a ring per step and, in branches, every detour and every step back. Before changing anything, you can see where someone got stuck.

  • Recommended route

    The turquoise axis: the shortest path the Guide verified, page by page.

  • Detours and steps back

    Each branch is a page off the route. Its thickness shows how many went through it, and its color how they ended.

  • ⚠

    Where they got stuck

    A mark where someone couldn't move forward: that's where the theme needs to help.

Replay

Every attempt, step by step

What the visitor saw, where they clicked, what they expected to find and where they ended up. The same evidence the Designer uses is in plain sight for anyone.

Download

Install Chameleon in your browser

Firefox

Available

The latest version, signed by Mozilla.

Download the .xpi

Firefox will ask for permission to install it: click Continue to Installation and then Add. If it downloads as a file, drag it onto a Firefox window. Requires Firefox 140 or later.

Chrome

Available

The latest version, to load in developer mode.

Download the .zip
  1. Unzip the .zip into a folder you won't delete.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose that folder.

Requires Chrome 148 or later. On the Chrome Web Store, coming soon.
